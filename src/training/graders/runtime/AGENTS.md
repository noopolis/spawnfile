# Grading runtime

Owns calibrated decisions, bounded projection escalation and result identity.
Public seam: index.ts. Providers are injected neutral contracts; never import
inference or CLI implementations, read historical labels, or open network routes.
Identity mismatch refuses before scoring. Forced teacher routes additionally
require stored independent teacher evidence (15 groups, 30 instances, 5 good/bad)
and agreement >= 0.85; absent metrics require recalibration. Teacher backends
retain their own verified teacher route even when an escalation teacher differs. Decisive failed mechanical gates return
fail with score zero before backend identity or inference calls. Missing evidence and overflow remain
missing. Every provider call must retain measured provenance and unknown usage.
Verify adjacent tests, four negative mutations, ESLint and per-file coverage.
The public grading-result schema preserves typed persisted identity, coverage and
question/gate results; callers supply their explicit judge-call schema. No passthrough
objects may bypass validation of persisted grading fields.
Contained tool-call rejections retain refused outcomes and typed tool-call detail in
judge-call evidence; they consume their call slot and contribute no answer.
Per-question calibration selects a named decision route. Probe all supplied routes
before and after scoring, and require a backend for every selected model. Preserve
single-model callers through the default route. Persist model IDs in decision
accounting without inventing provider calls; shared endpoint resources cover every
constructed model. Teacher selections keep their own route and bypass Platt.

Identity and score calls append dispatched receipts to a caller-owned collector.
Never derive invocation ownership from backend history or receipt offsets.
Paid grading failures retain invocation-scoped, allowlisted decision receipts on
DecisionAccountingError after started provider work settles; never attach request
payloads or provider response extensions to failure accounting.
Retain originating requestId in success, failure and persisted receipt schemas.
Assign once at invocation accounting for injected backends that omit it; never
replace existing IDs or derive identity from price, check or measurement names.

Expose one decisionAccountingSchema for successful and failed persisted results;
keep the same strict receipt allowlist at both replay surfaces.
